# Ozon Operations Playbook

Use this reference for broader seller operations beyond upload and keywords: pricing, logistics, promotions, analytics, finance, quality, returns, and practical troubleshooting.

## Operating Areas

When the user asks a broad Ozon question, map it to one or more areas:

| Area | Primary checks |
| --- | --- |
| Product eligibility | moderation, documents, category access, brand certificate, prohibited/restricted goods, duplicate PDP |
| PDP quality | category/type, required characteristics, filter attributes, description, images, variants |
| Availability | stock, warehouse, sales scheme, delivery geography, delivery speed |
| Price and economics | product price, discounts, commission, acquiring, delivery, returns, non-purchases, advertising, exchange rate |
| Search and analytics | search visibility, query fit, ranking factors, reviews, popularity, region/customer type |
| Promotion | Ozon promotions, seller promotions, paid search, pay-per-click/order tools, phrase reports |
| Customer experience | reviews, ratings, questions, returns, cancellations, quality metrics |
| Reporting | sales reports, service reports, mutual settlements, promotion analytics, search-query analytics |

## Pricing And Economics

Do not provide a single universal Ozon profit formula without asking for the sales scheme and current fee table.

Use this structure:

```text
gross revenue after seller discount
- Ozon sales fee / commission
- acquiring or payment-related charges if applicable
- delivery and fulfillment costs
- return, non-purchase, and cancellation costs
- advertising and promotion costs
- product cost
- first-mile / international logistics / customs / packaging
- exchange-rate and payout timing impact
= estimated profit
```

Important rules:

- Fees differ by seller country, category, sales scheme, and sometimes price tier/date.
- Ozon calculates sales fees on the seller-set price with seller discounts/promotions taken into account, excluding some Ozon point discount mechanics; verify current policy for the seller.
- Documents/reports in Finance can be needed to reconcile sales, services, compensation, and settlements.
- If the user wants exact profit, ask for category, scheme, seller country, selling price, cost, logistics cost, return rate, ad spend, current fee export, and exchange-rate rule.

## Logistics And Sales Schemes

Do not assume one universal fulfillment model.

Common Ozon Global seller concepts:

- realFBS: seller stores products and is responsible for delivery. The seller collects/packs orders and returns go back to seller warehouse.
- FBP/partner fulfillment: seller can place products in a fulfillment partner warehouse in China where applicable.
- FBO/FBS availability depends on country/region and seller program; verify current docs and account options.

When choosing a scheme, compare:

```text
delivery speed
geographic coverage
conversion impact
stock control
operational workload
return path
cost structure
category/product restrictions
cashflow and inventory risk
```

## Promotions And Ads

Separate these:

- Ozon promotions: platform-run or seasonal promotions; terms are in seller account.
- Seller promotions: seller-created discounts/promos.
- Paid product promotion: search/catalog/PDP placements, pay-per-click/order tools, phrase-based search ads where available.
- Brand promotion and display/media tools where eligible.

Before recommending ad scaling:

1. Confirm PDP eligibility and search visibility.
2. Check query relevance.
3. Compare price, reviews, delivery, images, and competitor PDPs.
4. Review phrase reports and negative words.
5. Calculate unit economics after ad cost.

## Troubleshooting Trees

### No impressions / no traffic

Check in order:

1. Product moderation/blocking/restricted category.
2. Product out of stock or warehouse unavailable for target region.
3. PDP errors or "requires improvement" state.
4. Search-index status in visibility tools.
5. Wrong category/type or missing filter characteristics.
6. Query/region/customer-type mismatch.
7. Price, promotion, delivery, and review disadvantage.

### Impressions but no clicks

Check:

- Main image quality and compliance.
- Customer-visible price and discount.
- Delivery promise and region.
- Rating/reviews versus competitors.
- Search query mismatch.
- Product name clarity and category fit.

### Clicks but no cart/orders

Check:

- PDP content completeness.
- Characteristics and compatibility clarity.
- Variant availability.
- Price and delivery competitiveness.
- Reviews/questions objections.
- Return/cancellation reasons.
- Promotion traffic quality.

### Ads spend but weak sales

Check:

- Whether the product was strong enough organically before ads.
- Phrase relevance and match waste.
- Negative words.
- Bid/budget versus expected margin.
- Competitor price/reviews/delivery.
- Cart and order conversion by query.
- Whether paid traffic is trying to fix a PDP/category problem.

### Upload passes but product still sells poorly

Check:

- Category/type accuracy.
- Characteristics used by filters.
- Search query fit.
- Region visibility.
- Delivery scheme and stock.
- Price tier.
- Reviews/popularity gap.
- Participation in relevant promotions.
